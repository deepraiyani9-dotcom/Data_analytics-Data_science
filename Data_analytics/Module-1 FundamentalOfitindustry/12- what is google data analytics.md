# What is Google Data Analytics?

Google Data Analytics is a professional field and learning program focused on using data to find useful insights and support business decisions.

## advantages of GDA

1. Easy to Learn – Suitable for beginners who are starting their data analytics career.
2. Career Opportunities – Helps prepare for jobs such as Data Analyst, Business Analyst, and Marketing Analyst.
3. Practical Skills – Teaches useful skills such as SQL, spreadsheets, data cleaning, analysis, and visualization.
4. Better Decision Making – Helps businesses make decisions based on data rather than guesses.
5. High Demand – Data analytics skills are useful across many industries.
6. Google Certification – The Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate can strengthen your resume.
7. Works With Real Data – You learn how to collect, clean, analyze, and present data.
8. Multiple Career Paths – Can be a foundation for advanced fields such as Data Science, Business Intelligence, and AI.
9. Remote Work Opportunities – Many data-related jobs can be performed remotely.
10. Good for Beginners – You don't need to be an advanced programmer to start learning data analytics.
