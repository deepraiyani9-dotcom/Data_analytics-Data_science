# what is LAN,WAN and MAN

  ## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)**

    - A network that connects computers and devices within a small area, such as a home, school, or office.
    - Example: Office computer network.

  ## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)**

    -A network that covers a larger area than LAN but smaller than WAN, usually a city or town.
-   -Example: A city's cable network.

  ## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)**

    - A network that covers a very large geographical area, such as countries or continents.
    - Example: The Internet.
