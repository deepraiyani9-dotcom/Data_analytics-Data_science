# what is seo ?

   - SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving a website to increase its visibility and ranking on search engine results pages (SERPs), such as Google.

## admvantages od seo

    -Increases website traffic.
    -Improves search engine ranking.
    -Increases brand awareness.
    -Provides better user experience.
    -Helps generate more leads and sales.
    -Builds credibility and trust.
    -Provides long-term marketing benefits.
    -Reduces advertising costs.
    -Helps reach the target audience.
